## 2. Mathematical Water Balance Formulation

HIDRIQ calculates water requirements on an individual zone-by-zone basis.

### 2.1 Zone Evapotranspiration Demand ($ET_c$)
$$ET_c = ET_0 \times K_c \times K_{mc} \times K_d$$
Where:
- $ET_0$: Daily reference evapotranspiration calculated via the ASCE Standardized Penman-Monteith equation (mm/day).
- $K_c$: Species-specific botanical crop coefficient for the active season.
- $K_{mc}$: Microclimate factor (accounting for solar azimuth, shade from structures, wind exposure, slope).
- $K_d$: Planting density / canopy closure factor.

### 2.2 Effective Rainfall ($P_{eff}$)
Small showers ($< 2.5\text{ mm}$) are intercepted by vegetation canopy and evaporate directly back to the atmosphere with zero subsurface infiltration. For rainfall $> 2.5\text{ mm}$, effective percolation is calculated via the USDA-SCS / FAO-56 empirical model:
$$P_{eff} = \max\left(0, P_{raw} \times 0.8 - 2.0\right) \quad (\text{for } P_{raw} \le 25\text{ mm})$$
For torrential events ($P_{raw} > 25\text{ mm}$), effective infiltration is capped by the soil's basic infiltration rate over the event duration to prevent false runoff accounting.

### 2.3 Physical Soil Water Reservoir
$$TAW = 1000 \times \left(\theta_{FC} - \theta_{WP}\right) \times Z_r$$
$$RAW = p \times TAW$$
Where:
- $TAW$: Total Available Water in the root zone (mm).
- $\theta_{FC}$: Soil moisture content at Field Capacity ($-33\text{ kPa}$, $\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$).
- $\theta_{WP}$: Soil moisture content at Permanent Wilting Point ($-1500\text{ kPa}$, $\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$).
- $Z_r$: Effective root depth of the specific plant species (m).
- $p$: Fraction of $TAW$ that can be depleted from the root zone before moisture stress occurs (e.g., $p=0.50$ for turf, $p=0.65$ for olive trees).
- $RAW$: Readily Available Water (mm).

### 2.4 Daily Root-Zone Water Depletion ($D_{r,t}$)
$$D_{r,t} = D_{r,t-1} + ET_c - P_{eff} - I_{net}$$
- If $D_{r,t} < 0$, soil moisture exceeds field capacity (drainage / deep percolation occurs, reset $D_{r,t} = 0$).
- If $D_{r,t} < RAW$: **NO IRRIGATION REQUIRED TODAY.** Soil moisture reserve remains within optimal buffer.
- If $D_{r,t} \ge RAW$: **IRRIGATION TRIGGERED.** The zone has reached the allowable depletion threshold.

### 2.5 Gross Irrigation Volume & Runtime
$$I_{net} = D_{r,t}$$
$$I_{gross} = \frac{I_{net}}{\eta_a}$$
$$\text{Volume (Liters)} = I_{gross} \times \text{Zone Area } (\text{m}^2)$$

$$\text{Runtime (Minutes)} = \left(\frac{I_{gross}}{P_r}\right) \times 60 \quad (\text{ONLY calculated if } P_r > 0)$$

> **CRITICAL RULE:** If precipitation rate ($P_r$) is unknown, HIDRIQ returns:  
> *"Water requirement estimated ($X\text{ mm}$), irrigation runtime cannot yet be calculated reliably without precipitation rate."*  
> The system never fabricates unmeasured hardware precision.

## 5. Mandatory 8-Point Explainability Report

Every recommendation generated by the HIDRIQ engine answers:
1. **Why is water required?** (Or why is water NOT required, e.g. deep olive root buffer).
2. **How much water is required?** (Depth in mm and volume in Liters across zone surface area).
3. **Why now?** (VPD conditions, solar radiation, pre-dawn 05:30–07:00 AM window to eliminate evaporative drift).
4. **Why this zone specifically?** (Contrasting root depth, drought tolerance, and transpirational sensitivity).
5. **What data supports this recommendation?** (Explicit telemetry provenance table).
6. **What assumptions were made?** (Root depth, soil texture, distribution uniformity, microclimate factor).
7. **How confident is HIDRIQ?** (Composite score breaking down vegetation, soil, irrigation, and weather certainty).
8. **What would change this recommendation?** (Rain interlock $>5\text{ mm}$, temperature drops, in-situ soil tension probes).

## 6. Strict Data Provenance Classification

Every metric displayed in the platform bears one of 5 mandatory provenance badges:
- **`MEASURED`**: Directly acquired via calibrated physical sensors, utility pulse meters, or on-site rain gauges.
- **`USER_CONFIRMED`**: Confirmed by homeowner or commercial operator via interactive prompt or ground-truth edit.
- **`INFERRED`**: Derived from high-resolution spatial models, cadastral boundaries, or microclimate grids.
- **`ESTIMATED`**: Calculated via regional agronomic averages or default soil surveys.
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Data is unavailable; the system explicitly refuses to guess or fabricate precision.
