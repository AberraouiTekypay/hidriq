## 2. Parallel Dual-Track Launch Stages

### Stage 01: Parallel Launch [NOW]
*Commercial Hospitality (Morocco) + Consumer Garden Intelligence (Spain)*

#### Track A: HIDRIQ BUSINESS (Beachhead: Morocco)
- **Design Partner:** **Zephyr Hotels (Morocco)** — 60-day phased commercial POC.
- **Phasing:** Shadow Mode baseline audit $\rightarrow$ Daily groundskeeper run-sheets $\rightarrow$ Selective autopilot on supported test zones.
- **Engine Validation:** ASCE FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference $ET_0$, Landscape Coefficients ($K_L$), sub-meter flow reconciliation, and leak anomaly detection.

#### Track B: HIDRIQ HOME (Beachhead: Spain)
- **Target Launch Geography:** Spanish Mediterranean markets: **Costa del Sol, Marbella, Estepona, Sotogrande, Mallorca, Alicante, Valencia, and Murcia**.
- **Core Product:** **Manual Garden Mode & Living Digital Twin** — self-service onboarding where homeowners confirm automated property discovery and upload targeted photos to receive weekly personalized watering schedules without requiring smart controllers or hardware.
- **Traction Objective:** Initial validation sprint targeting **1,000+ gardens assessed** to prove organic acquisition, recommendation quality, activation, and free-to-paid conversion.

---

### Stage 02: Scale & Portfolio Expansion [NEXT]
*Golf Courses & Mediterranean Residential Expansion*

- **B2B Expansion:** Onboarding championship golf courses across Morocco and Southern Spain, modeling complex turf topographies, elevation slope cycle-and-soak execution, and off-peak electrical tariff pumping.
- **B2C Expansion:** Broadening HIDRIQ HOME to Portugal (Algarve, Cascais), Italy (Tuscany, Sardinia), Greece, and the French Riviera.
- **Absentee Second-Home Monitoring:** Dedicated alert engine notifying absentee homeowners of freeze risks, burst pipes, and rain-adjusted watering pauses.
- **Controller Connectors:** API adapters for cloud-enabled residential and commercial controllers (Hunter Hydrawise, Rain Bird IQ4, Toro Horizon360).

---

### Stage 03: High-Value Specialty Agriculture [COMING SOON]
*From Landscape Aesthetics to Economic Yield Optimization*

- **Focus:** Water-intensive, high-value perennial crops in arid and semi-arid basins (olives, citrus, almonds, avocados, table grapes, dates).
- **Agronomic Hierarchy:** Activation of the multi-tier agricultural schema:
  $$\text{Farm} \longrightarrow \text{Field} \longrightarrow \text{Block} \longrightarrow \text{Crop} \longrightarrow \text{Variety} \longrightarrow \text{Growth Stage} \longrightarrow \text{Soil Hydrology}$$
- **Objective Function Evolution:** Transition from landscape conservation (minimizing liters applied) to **maximizing economic yield value per cubic metre ($/m^3)**.
- **Sensor Fusion:** Ingestion of optional subsurface capacitance probes and dendrometers to calibrate depletion curves.

---

### Stage 04: Water Infrastructure & Basin Intelligence [FUTURE]
*District-Scale Water Intelligence*

- **Focus:** Municipal water utilities, regional irrigation districts, agricultural cooperatives, and smart city infrastructure.
- **District Demand Aggregation:** Basin-scale predictive modeling enabling water authorities to forecast aggregate withdrawal demand 72 hours in advance.
- **Non-Revenue Water (NRW) Localization:** Network-wide acoustic and hydraulic anomaly detection to pinpoint distribution losses in municipal supply conduits.
- **Vendor-Neutral Policy Orchestration:** Macro-level drought restriction enforcement through centralized API rules applied across heterogeneous downstream controllers.
